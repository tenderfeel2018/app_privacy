隐私政策

RadioCalculator — 最后更新：2026-09-28

不收集个人数据 无广告 无第三方 SDK 无数据跟踪

1. 我们收集什么

本应用不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人数据或可识别设备信息。 所有计算均在你的设备本地完成，不需要网络连接，也不向任何服务器上传内容。

2. 应用内购买

应用内购买（一次性买断 RadioCalculator Pro）通过 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 官方渠道结算。 支付信息、账单地址等数据由对应商店直接处理，本应用不接触、不存储这些信息。 关于商店自身的隐私政策，请参考： Apple 隐私政策、 Google 隐私政策。

3. 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方 SDK，不使用任何数据分析、崩溃收集、 广告或行为追踪服务。也不通过 Google Analytics、Firebase、 AppsFlyer 等工具收集用户行为数据。

4. 系统权限

本应用仅在 Android 平台请求网络权限（用于 Google Play 结算回调）； 不请求位置、通讯录、相册、麦克风、通知等与功能无关的权限。 应用运行所需的系统级 API（如检查磁盘空间、读取设备启动时间） 不会暴露你的身份或被用于追踪。

5. 数据安全

因为我们不收集任何数据，所以不存在数据泄露、滥用或删除的风险。

6. 儿童隐私

本应用面向无线电工程从业者及爱好者， 不针对 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不主动收集儿童数据。

7. 适用地区 / 合规

本应用在全球可用，不向任何地区的监管机构上传数据。 如适用 GDPR、CCPA、LGPD 等法规，因本应用不处理个人数据， 这些法规下的数据主体权利请求不适用于本应用。

8. 政策变更

如本隐私政策发生任何变更（增加数据收集方式、改变用途等）， 我们将在应用内弹窗提醒，并更新本文档的「最后更新」日期。 持续使用本应用即视为同意更新后的政策。

Privacy Policy

RadioCalculator — Last updated: September 28, 2026

No personal data collected No ads No 3rd‑party SDKs No tracking

1. What we collect

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or device‑identifiable information. All calculations run entirely on your device. The app works without a network connection and does not send any user content or device data to any server.

2. In‑app purchases

Purchases within the app (one‑time permanent unlock of RadioCalculator Pro) are processed exclusively through Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment details, billing addresses and related data are handled directly by the respective store; we never see or store that information. For the stores' own privacy policies see: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

3. Third‑party services

This app does not embed any third‑party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting, advertising or behavioral tracking libraries. It does not use Google Analytics, Firebase, AppsFlyer or similar tools to collect user behavior data.

4. System permissions

On Android the app requests the network permission only (required for Google Play billing callbacks). We do not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications, or any other permission unrelated to the core calculator functionality. System APIs used by the app (for example checking disk space or device uptime) cannot identify you and are not used for tracking.

5. Data security

Since we do not collect any data, there is no risk of data leakage, misuse or deletion to protect against.

6. Children's privacy

RadioCalculator is intended for radio engineering professionals and hobbyists. It is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Jurisdiction / compliance

The app is available worldwide and we do not upload data to any regional authority. To the extent that GDPR, CCPA, LGPD or similar regulations apply, they do not give rise to data‑subject requests against this app because we do not process personal data.

8. Policy changes

If this privacy policy is changed in any material way (for example, a new method of data collection is introduced), we will show an in‑app notice and update the "Last updated" date on this page. Continuing to use the app after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
